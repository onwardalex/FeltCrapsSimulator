Felt: Craps Simulator Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Felt: Craps Simulator has no accounts and no real-money purchases. Your game progress stays on your phone. The app shows optional ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device and ad-related data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

This policy explains how the Felt: Craps Simulator app for iPhone ("the app"), made by Alex Moore ("I", "me"), handles information. If you have questions, email onwardalex@gmail.com.

Information the app stores on your device

The app saves your game progress on your phone: your sim-chip bankroll, bets on the table, the dice styles you own and use, and your settings (such as haptics). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to me or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sim chips are play money. They have no cash value and can't be bought, sold or redeemed.

Information I collect

None. I don't run servers for the app, I don't have user accounts, and I don't receive analytics or personal information from the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short ad to receive sim chips. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When the app requests or shows an ad, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version;
  • an identifier for advertising (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • an app-specific device identifier used for fraud prevention and frequency capping;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were watched or tapped;
  • diagnostic data about ad performance and crashes in the ad software.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you've allowed it, personalize them. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA isn't shared and ads aren't personalized based on tracking. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: players in these regions are shown Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. You can review or change your choice any time in the app under Settings > Privacy > Privacy choices.
  • Watching ads is optional. Your choices about tracking and consent never change the chips you receive for watching an ad.

Children

The app is a simulated casino game rated for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. I hold no data about you to delete. For data Google collects through ads, see Google's Privacy Policy and your Google ad settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date.
