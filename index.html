Felt: Craps Simulator

Learn craps and play it with sim chips on iPhone.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email onwardalex@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

Can I win real money? No. Sim chips are play money with no cash value. They can't be bought, sold or redeemed.

I ran out of chips. In Play mode, tap the + next to your bankroll to watch a short optional ad for more.

Where is my progress saved? Only on your phone. Deleting the app resets it.
